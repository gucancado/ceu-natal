# Birth chart

1. Ask for the date, time and city of birth in one question. The time and city are optional, but without them there are no houses or angles.
2. Call `calcular_mapa_natal` with the date as DD/MM/YYYY and the time as HH:MM.
3. Use the returned positions as they are. Do not estimate or recalculate degrees, signs, houses or aspects.
4. Give a short symbolic reading, then mention what else can be calculated: transits, synastry, composite chart, progressions and solar or lunar returns.

Astrology here is symbolic, for entertainment and self-reflection. Do not use a chart to support medical, legal or financial decisions.
